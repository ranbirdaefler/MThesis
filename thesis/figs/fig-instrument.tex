\caption[How NIR and the scramble ablation work]{\textbf{Every drug-use claim in this thesis is the
difference between two byte-identical prompts scored against one shared truth.} (a) $\NIR$ asks
whether a prediction resembles its own condition's truth more than it resembles the truths of the
other drugs in the same comparison set, so the generic response --- present in every truth equally
--- cancels, and only the drug-specific difference decides. Chance at $0.50$ is therefore a statement
about the comparison set, not about the model; and a predictor whose similarities are all identical
scores $0.50$ rather than $0$, which is why the tie term is not cosmetic. (b) The scramble replaces
the drug name and mechanism and nothing else. Because both arms are conditioned on the same control
cell and scored against the same truth, control- and batch-derived leakage enters both equally and
cancels in the difference --- which is why $\gap$, and not a comparison against a drug-agnostic
predictor, is the quantity every claim here is anchored to.}
