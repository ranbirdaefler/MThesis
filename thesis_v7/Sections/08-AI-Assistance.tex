\markboth{Statement on AI assistance}{Statement on AI assistance}

AI tools, including OpenAI Codex~\cite{openai_codex} and Anthropic
Claude~\cite{anthropic_claude}, were used to assist with formatting, wording and
rephrasing parts of the text, and creating figures for
visualisation. Further uses included generating scripts for submitting jobs to
high-performance computing (HPC) systems, preparing documentation, bibliographic
checks, and selected consistency checks of code and reported results. The author
remains responsible for the final text, figures, analyses and conclusions.
